## Definitieve prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: photographic hero asset for an interactive before/after website design example of a premium local auto detailing studio. Generate a cinematic high-end automotive campaign photograph, wide landscape 1536x1024 composition. A beautiful unbranded graphite-grey performance coupe in an immaculate pitch-dark detailing studio, three-quarter front angle, camera near headlight height, sculpted glossy metal, warm ivory linear studio lights reflected in the bodywork, subtle warm amber reflections. The car occupies the RIGHT two-thirds of the frame, front nose points toward the bottom left; entire car silhouette is visible with comfortable breathing room. Left third transitions into nearly solid charcoal black #111214 usable as clean negative space for website copy. Dramatic restrained product photography with expensive materials and realistic tires and glass, premium editorial precision, no fog or smoke, no showroom clutter, no people, no visible brand badges, no logos, no license-plate lettering, no words or UI, no watermarks. Strong clean silhouette and careful highlight detail rather than a pitch-black barely visible car.
